Welcome to the Animal Anti-Cruelty League

The Animal Anti-Cruelty League(AACL) is an animal welfare organisation dedicated to protecting animals from cruelty, neglect and abuse.

The organisation provides services such as animal shelter, adoption, veterinary care, animal cruelty investigations, education and community outreach.

What We Do

Animal Shelter

We provide care and shelter for animals that have been abandoned, rescued or surrendered.

Animal Adoption

We help animals find safe and loving permanent homes through responsible adoption

Veterinary Care

Veterinary services help improve the health and wellbeing of animals.

How Can You Help

You can make a difference by adopting an animal, volunteering, donating or supporting animal welfare.
